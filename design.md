# Transfer Guard design system
A restrained fintech operations console, with a persistent left sidebar, a compact utility topbar and a two-column, single-screen workspace. Deep ink navy typography (#152339), teal accent (#097970), muted blue-gray metadata, frost-gray canvas (#F5F7FA), white surfaces, and 1px cool borders (#E0E6ED). Green, amber and red convey policy outcomes, paired with text and icons. Use DM Sans for UI, Manrope for headings, and IBM Plex Mono for session/reference identifiers. A terraced type scale (32 / 17 / 14 / 12), consistent 4/8px spacing, 12px primary panel radius, and subtle shadows keep the interface calm. Lucide line icons; decorative shield/rings rendered as lightweight SVG. Main views: Simulator, Audit log, and Policy rules. Chat is the main surface, audit is the narrower companion. Examples populate the composer. New session starts a clean, private workspace. Mobile stacks panels and turns sidebar into a compact nav. Provide visible focus, semantic regions, reduced-motion handling and explicit simulation language throughout.
